Comcast Business · Hero Digital

Send-frequency governance for 14M+ contacts

14M+contacts, one model

Problem

Dozens of always-on campaigns each kept their own exclusion rules, so no one could see how much email a contact received in total.

Solution

Six engagement stages with touch caps, separate tiers for prospects and customers, every campaign mapped in, and segment counts modeled before launch.

Result

One auditable system replaced fragile per-campaign logic. It also showed stakeholders how stacking holiday sends of 300K–600K customers each would hurt the upsell, and one send was cut.

Built with Oracle Eloqua, segmentation modeling, suppression logic

Deliverability and compliance turnaround

+10%deliverability in one quarter

Problem

Solution

Deliverability up 10% within a quarter. Engagement stabilized and confidence in the metrics came back.

Built with SPF, DKIM, DMARC, suppression logic
