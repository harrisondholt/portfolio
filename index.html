Hero Digital · Senior Marketing Automation Specialist

Lead scoring realignment with sales

+15% MQL-to-SQL

Problem

Lifecycle stages looked right on paper, but MQL-to-SQL conversion was inconsistent. Sales wasn’t qualifying leads the way the scoring model assumed, and the disagreement over lead quality was turning into friction.

Solution

Instead of debating whose numbers were right, I asked sales to review the conversion data with me. Side by side, it showed the model was too generous in how far leads could progress and was inflating early-stage engagement. I tightened the scoring thresholds, aligned stage definitions with how sales qualifies, and simplified nurture paths that had grown around the old thresholds.

Result

MQL-to-SQL conversion rose 15%. Over the following quarter, SQL acceptance stabilized and funnel reporting became trustworthy again. It wasn’t a flashy project, but it made the funnel predictable.

What changed

  1. Scoring thresholdsTightened so leads progress only when they look ready.
  2. Stage definitionsAligned with how sales actually qualifies.
  3. Nurture pathsSimplified where they had grown around loose thresholds.
  4. SQL acceptanceStabilized, and conversion up 15%.

Comcast Business · Hero Digital

Send-frequency governance for 14M+ contacts

14M+ contacts, 1 model

Problem

Dozens of always-on campaigns ran at once, and each kept its own exclusion rules. No campaign could see the total volume a contact received. A contact could be excluded from one campaign and still be hit by three others in the same week.

Solution

Defined five engagement stages, from Active 1 to Inactive, each with its own touch caps. Built separate tier systems for prospects and customers, since cadence and rules differ. Mapped every always-on campaign into the framework and modeled segment counts before launch. Then retrofitted the platform and monitored send counts against the model.

Result

One central, auditable system replaced fragile per-campaign logic. Send volume per contact became predictable and enforceable, and adding a campaign meant slotting it into the framework. The same model showed stakeholders that a stack of holiday sends would lock a few hundred thousand customers out of a revenue-driving upsell, and one send was cut to protect it.

Comcast Business · Hero Digital

Deliverability and compliance turnaround

+10% deliverability

Problem

A client’s email deliverability was declining. It was hurting campaign performance and undermining confidence in the reporting.

Solution

Ran a full deliverability audit, then implemented SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication. Strengthened suppression logic, formalized list-hygiene standards, and added QA checkpoints to keep sends compliant with CAN-SPAM and internal governance.

Result

Deliverability improved 10% within a quarter. Engagement stabilized, compliance risk dropped, and confidence in campaign performance metrics was restored.

Comcast Business · Hero Digital

Customer onboarding journey redesign

Many entry points to 1 feed

Problem

The onboarding nurture program had numerous entry points, some undocumented, and siloed work streams. There were no clear lifecycle triggers, and messaging was inconsistent across touchpoints, which contributed to early-stage drop-off.

Solution

Partnered with data and product stakeholders to consolidate onboarding data into a single-source feed. Rebuilt the program in Eloqua so email sequencing, CRM lifecycle stages, and internal notifications work as one experience. Closed duplicate and unknown entry points, and added clearer segmentation rules and testing protocols for cadence and timing.

Result

Early engagement improved through better relevance and timing. Internal handoffs became more consistent, the program scaled across segments, and leadership gained clearer visibility into early lifecycle performance.
