Comcast Business · Hero Digital

Dynamic upsell and cross-sell campaign

~15%more customer upgrades, 25% average open rate

Problem

Existing customers got static-list outreach instead of messages driven by behavior or firmographic signals. That limited expansion revenue.

Solution

  • Worked with the data team so behavioral and firmographic data flowed into Eloqua.
  • Built a four-email campaign whose decision logic surfaces the products each customer is most likely to upgrade to.
  • Made it modular, so products and offers can change without disrupting the rest of the workflow.
  • Aligned messaging with Customer Success, gave sales visibility, and owned segmentation, QA, deployment, and analysis.

Result

A 25% average open rate and roughly 15% more customer upgrades, ahead of quarterly engagement targets. Marketing and post-sale teams were better aligned.

Built with Oracle Eloqua, dynamic content, behavioral and firmographic data

Hero Digital

Lead scoring realignment with sales

+15%MQL-to-SQL conversion

Problem

Lifecycle stages looked right on paper, but MQL-to-SQL conversion was inconsistent. Sales wasn’t qualifying leads the way the scoring model assumed, and the disagreement over lead quality was creating friction.

Solution

  • Asked sales to review the conversion data with me instead of debating whose numbers were right.
  • Found the model was too generous and was inflating early-stage engagement.
  • Tightened scoring thresholds and aligned stage definitions with how sales actually qualifies.
  • Simplified nurture paths that had grown around the old thresholds.

Result

Conversion up 15%. SQL acceptance stabilized over the following quarter and funnel reporting became trustworthy again.

Built with Lead scoring, lifecycle stages, segmentation

Comcast Business · Hero Digital

Send-frequency governance for 14M+ contacts

14M+contacts, one model

Problem

Dozens of always-on campaigns each kept their own exclusion rules, so no campaign could see the total volume a contact received. A contact could be excluded from one and still be hit by three others in the same week.

Solution

  • Defined six engagement stages, each with its own touch caps.
  • Built separate tier systems for prospects and customers.
  • Mapped every always-on campaign into the framework.
  • Modeled segment counts before launch, then monitored send counts after rollout.

Result

One auditable system replaced fragile per-campaign logic across 14M+ contacts, and new campaigns slot into the framework. It also showed stakeholders how stacking holiday sends of 300K–600K customers each would hurt the upsell, and one send was cut.

Built with Oracle Eloqua, segmentation modeling, suppression logic

Comcast Business · Hero Digital

Deliverability and compliance turnaround

+10%deliverability in one quarter

Problem

A client’s email deliverability was declining. It was hurting campaign performance and undermining confidence in the reporting.

Solution

  • Ran a full deliverability audit to find the root cause.
  • Implemented SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication.
  • Strengthened suppression logic and formalized list-hygiene standards.
  • Added QA checkpoints to keep sends compliant with CAN-SPAM and internal governance.

Result

Deliverability improved 10% within a quarter. Engagement stabilized, compliance risk dropped, and confidence in the campaign metrics came back.

Built with SPF, DKIM, DMARC, suppression logic
